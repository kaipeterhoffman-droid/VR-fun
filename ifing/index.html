Trick Shot

ÍFING

“Ífing the river is called, that parts the land of the giants’ sons from the gods’;
open it shall run through all the days: no ice comes on that river.”

A boat, a lantern and a river through a forest out of the old North. Made for Quest: sit down, reach forward and take an oar in each hand with the grip or the trigger, dip the blades and push them away from you. You face the way she goes. Let go and the oars trail beside her. On a desktop, drag to look, and the arrow keys row. There is sound, all of it made by the page itself; M mutes it.
